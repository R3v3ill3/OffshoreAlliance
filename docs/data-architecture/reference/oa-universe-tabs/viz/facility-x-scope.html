Legend & method notes (from the tab itself)

  • LEGEND & METHOD
  • Cell shade = best confidence in the cell (green H / amber M / pink L); BOLD = contains a [WB] whiteboard or [MV] member-verified link
  • WHO HOLDS EACH SCOPE AT EACH FACILITY — the colleague's 'facility × catering × maintenance' format generalised to 14 scope columns (structure agreed with Troy 22 Sep 2026). Rows = your Key Assets (plus three decommissioning assets not on that tab, shaded orange). Each cell lists the incumbent(s) with confidence and source tag: [WB] colleague whiteboard, [MV] member/Troy-verified, untagged = desk research.
  • HOW IT'S BUILT: derived from the contractor linkages (company → asset) by assigning each company its scope, with per-asset overrides where a company plays a different role at a specific facility (Monadelphous = HUC at Scarborough & Crux but Maintenance on the NWS platforms; McDermott = Construction at Scarborough but Decommissioning at Stybarrow). MODEC's self-performed O&M/catering at Pyrenees is shown explicitly; Tier-1 and facility/vessel operator are both shown (cols A–B).
  • NORMALLY-UNMANNED INSTALLATIONS (Angel, Pluto A, John Brookes, Crux, Reindeer): no standing caterer is mapped — during extended maintenance/repair campaigns the accommodation and catering arrangements are project-specific (Troy, 22 Sep 2026).
  • RECOMMENDED USE: maintain the catering & maintenance incumbents here (and the other scopes). Treat each cell as 'current incumbent(s)'; when a contract changes, update the linkages tab and regenerate, or edit here and note the date. Crewing / labour-hire firms have their own column because they sit BEHIND the incumbent (e.g. AOS crews on Saipem's Castorone).
  • DATA-MODEL NOTE for the OA app: store as a long table (facility, scope, company, confidence, source, as-at date) and pivot to this matrix.
  • Colour key on the tab: High · Medium · Low
