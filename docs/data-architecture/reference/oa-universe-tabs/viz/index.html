How to read these pages

  • Confidence follows the workbook’s own scale, shown as a chip on every page: H high — a documented contract or enterprise agreement; M medium — company or industry evidence, with the facility or currency unconfirmed; L low — inferred or historic only.
  • A WB tag marks a fact that came from the colleague’s whiteboard matrix rather than desk research.
  • An empty matrix cell means no linkage recorded — not that none exists. The reconciliation tab tracks the known gaps.
  • Charts bucket the free-text status, confidence and presence columns by their leading term, so “Operating (first gas Feb 2025)” counts as Operating. The full text is always in the table underneath.
  • Every page has a searchable table of the underlying rows; columns with no values on a tab are hidden behind a toggle.
  • These pages are generated. Edit the CSVs, then re-run python3 scripts/data-hygiene/oa-universe/build_tab_visualisations.py.
